## Verification

Google usually asks a service-area business for a short continuous video: show the location sign or mailing address,
proof you run the business (LLC documents, a business bank statement header, or tools you use at work), and that you
manage it (for example open the website admin or an invoice). Have these ready before you start. Verification can take
a few days.
